Why this connector

A store owner should not have to check the sync.

Most connector trouble is quiet: an order that imported twice, a total that is a few cents out, stock that drifted, a payout nobody matched. This one is built so that none of that can happen silently. Each order is compared with the Shopify total before it is confirmed, each webhook is verified and processed once, stock is sent with Shopify's own committed quantities taken into account, and anything Odoo cannot do is stopped with the reason in a sentence and a Retry button, not buried in a log.

Correct before fast

Totals checked on every order

Taxes, discounts, shipping and rounding are rebuilt in Odoo and compared with the Shopify total. A mismatch stays a quotation and the Shopify order is marked Needs attention with the exact difference, never a quietly wrong invoice.

Accounting that closes

Payments and payouts that reconcile

Each Shopify payment is registered on the journal you choose. Payouts post their fees and settle those payments, then wait for the bank deposit that matches them, so the clearing account empties.

Built to be left alone

Work that survives anything

A durable job queue survives restarts, retries with backoff, keeps each Shopify record in order and stays inside Shopify cost limits. Webhooks do the work and scheduled polling covers any gap.

Totals that match

Every order checked against Shopify, to the cent.

Shopify on the left, Odoo on the rightEach order keeps the Shopify total, the Odoo total and the difference side by side, with its lines, shipments, refunds and payments one click away and the sales order behind it.

Money in the bank

Payouts reconciled, fees booked, deposits matched.

One payout, every charge accounted forCharges, refunds, adjustments and fees come from Shopify Payments. The payout posts one entry for its fees and settles each order's payment, then matches the deposit when it reaches your bank statement.

What it keeps in step

Everything a Shopify store runs on, kept in step with Odoo.

Orders

Sales orders, invoices, payments

Orders arrive with taxes, discounts and shipping. Customers match by link, exact email or phone before a contact is created, and B2B orders keep their company and payment terms.

Catalogue

Products linked, never guessed

Link existing products by SKU or barcode, create the rest from Shopify, or publish Odoo products up to Shopify, with images and mapped metafields.

Fulfillment

Ship from Odoo or Shopify

Validating a delivery sends tracking to Shopify, and shipments made in Shopify come back. Stock goes out per location, with units promised to orders counted once.

Markets

Price lists that follow Odoo

Market and B2B price lists take their prices from an Odoo pricelist, with compare at prices and quantity price breaks when you want them.

Stock that tells the truth

Each Shopify location offered what it really holds.

Every unit counted onceEach Shopify location maps to its own Odoo warehouse. Units Odoo already holds for imported orders are added back, then the units Shopify has committed to those same orders are taken off, so nothing is counted twice. A row that changes in Shopify while it is being sent is flagged, not overwritten.

Nothing silent

Every piece of work is a job you can see, retry or cancel.

The whole sync, in one listEvery import, push and payout is a job with its attempts, timing and result. Jobs for the same Shopify record run in order, and failures wait in one exceptions inbox.

The reason in a sentence, then RetryA problem on the Odoo side is shown as Odoo said it and is not retried eight times. Fix the cause, press Retry, and the order continues where it stopped.

Once and only once

Webhooks that cannot double post

Every delivery is checked against the shop secret, stored with its Shopify id and processed once. A webhook delivered twice, late or out of order changes nothing it should not.

Sealed credentials

Secrets never stored in clear

Client secrets and tokens are encrypted before they reach the database, with a key you can keep outside it. A key that no longer matches is reported in a sentence, not a server error.

Honest boundaries

What it does, and what it does not.

It installs Sales, Inventory, Invoicing and Delivery with it, because the order to cash loop needs all four, and the server needs the requests and cryptography Python libraries, both standard on a normal Odoo install. It runs on premise and on Odoo.sh; Odoo Online cannot run third party modules, so it cannot run there.

Payouts, disputes, gift cards and orders older than 60 days need Shopify permissions that Shopify grants only to an approved app or on a plan that carries them. Until the app has them those features stay off, and the store's health panel says which ones.

SMS marketing consent is not mirrored; email consent is. Gift card balances from before the cut over are booked as one opening amount, not per card. Returns receive restocked units, and damaged units too when you name a location for them; units Shopify records as missing are cancelled. Quantity edits can be sent from Odoo only before the order ships or is invoiced and while no refund is waiting to be recorded; otherwise edit the order in Shopify.
