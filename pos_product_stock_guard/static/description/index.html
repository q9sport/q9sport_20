POS Stock Display & Block

Shows the stock left on every POS product card, and warns or blocks the cashier before an order asks for more than you have.

The situation

The register shows a name, a picture and a price, but not how many units are on the shelf. A cashier sells 3 Monitor Stands when WH/Stock holds 1, the delivery goes negative, and someone has to call the customer back. To check, the cashier opens the product info popup for every item or asks the back office.

The module puts the available quantity in a corner of each storable product card, green when there is stock and red at zero or below. Each point of sale chooses which quantity to show, which location to read, and whether an order that asks for more is allowed, confirmed or refused.

What it adds

Stock on Product Cards
Stock badge on the card
A small badge in the top-left corner of the card shows what is left. It drops as lines are added to any open order on the register and turns red at 0.
Without it: Standard Odoo shows stock only inside the product info popup.
Quantity Shown
On Hand, Free to Use or Forecasted
On Hand is what is in the location now. Free to Use takes off what is already reserved for other transfers. Forecasted adds incoming and removes outgoing transfers.
Stock Location
Location per point of sale
Leave it empty to read the source location of the Operation Type, sub-locations included. Pick another internal location for a shop that sells from its own room.
Out-of-Stock Products
Allow, Warn or Block
Warn opens a 'Not enough stock' dialog with Add anyway and Cancel. Block shows 'Out of stock' and the product is not added.
Without it: Standard Odoo adds the line and lets the stock go negative.
Numpad quantity
Raising a line is checked too
In Block mode, typing 5 on a line of 2 when only 2 are in stock is refused. In Warn mode a notification tells the cashier how many are left.
Can Sell Out of Stock
Manager override
Users of the chosen group get the confirmation instead of the block. It starts as Point of Sale / Administrator; leave it empty so nobody can override.
Services and consumables
Only storable products count
Services, consumables and combo headers get no badge and are never blocked, so a delivery fee or a gift card always goes through.

Getting started

Install

Open Apps, clear the Apps filter, search for POS Stock Display & Block and click Activate. On Odoo 20 it needs PoS - Stock, the link between Point of Sale and Inventory. Close and reopen the POS session afterwards so the register reloads its files.

Apps › pos_product_stock_guard › Activate
Configure

Open the settings of a point of sale and go to the Inventory block. Tick Stock on Product Cards, pick the Quantity Shown and, if needed, a Stock Location. Then choose what happens with Out-of-Stock Products and, for Block, who Can Sell Out of Stock. Every setting is per point of sale.

Point of Sale › Configuration › Settings › Inventory
Use

Open the register as usual. The quantities are read once when the register loads, and the register subtracts what it rings up from then on. Click a product with nothing left and the cashier sees the dialog of the mode you chose.

Block mode: Monitor Stand has 1 unit and it is already on the order, so a second one is refused.
Warn mode, or Block for a POS Administrator: the cashier can still add Office Lamp after confirming.

Fine print

When are the quantities refreshed?
When the register loads. After that the register lowers them itself for every line it rings up, paid or not. Reload the POS to read the stock again, for example after a delivery came in.
Do two registers see each other's sales?
Only after a reload. Each register reads the stock when it starts and then counts its own orders, so two tills selling the last unit at the same minute can both sell it.
What happens offline?
If the register can't reach the server to read a quantity, that product has no badge and is never blocked. Selling never stops because a figure is missing.
Where can I run it?
On Odoo.sh and on your own server, Community or Enterprise, versions 17.0 to 20.0. Odoo Online doesn't accept third-party modules.
What doesn't it do?
It does not reserve stock or change any stock move. Quantities are read when the register loads, not live. The items picked inside a combo are not checked. Configurable and weighed products are checked once the variant or the weight is known. With the 'at session closing' inventory setting, orders already paid in the open session are not in the server figure after a reload. The override uses a user group, not the POS employee login.
